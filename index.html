Import ratings

Paste a CSV with team, offense, defense and tempo (adjusted, per 100 possessions). Exports from Bart Torvik's T-Rank (free) and KenPom (subscription) are recognized automatically. A plain Team,Off,Def,Tempo list works too.

Model settings

Defaults: home court ≈ 3 pts, σ ≈ 11 for margins and ≈ 15 for totals (typical D-I game-to-game spread). Leave league averages blank to use the average of your loaded teams. A Kelly fraction of 0.25 (quarter Kelly) is the usual safer stake size.

The model

Hoops Oracle uses the tempo-free efficiency approach behind the leading public college basketball systems (KenPom, Bart Torvik). Each team gets three numbers: points scored per 100 possessions (offense), points allowed per 100 possessions (defense), and possessions per game (tempo), all adjusted for the opponents it played.

Projecting a game

  1. Pace: expected possessions = Tempo A × Tempo B ÷ league tempo.
  2. Efficiency: A's points per 100 = A offense × B defense ÷ league average. Same for B.
  3. Score: efficiency × possessions ÷ 100, then home court (± half each side) and any lineup adjustments you enter.
  4. Win probability: margins are roughly normally distributed around the projection, so P(win) = Φ(margin ÷ σ), with σ ≈ 11 points.

Finding value

Enter a sportsbook line and the app compares the model's probability with the break-even probability implied by the odds (52.4% at −110). A positive edge means the model rates that bet above what the price requires. Stake sizes follow fractional Kelly.

Building your own ratings

The Build tab fits ratings from raw scores with an iterative opponent adjustment (a ridge regression on points scored and allowed). It discounts older games, caps blowouts at 25 points, pulls teams with few games toward average, and learns home-court advantage and σ from your data. The walk-forward backtest reports accuracy, Brier score and calibration so you can tune the settings honestly.

Getting data

  • Ratings (easiest): on barttorvik.com, copy the ratings table (team, AdjOE, AdjDE, Adj T.) into the Team ratings tab. Re-import every few days; ratings move a lot early in the season.
  • Game results: any results export with dates, teams and scores works in the Build tab, or keep your own spreadsheet and paste it in.
  • Team names must match between your ratings and the games you predict. Matching ignores capitals and punctuation.

Using it well

  • Use the lineup adjustment for injuries or suspensions. Losing a star is often worth 2–5 points.
  • Early season (November), ratings are noisy. Lean on preseason-informed ratings like Torvik's until teams have 8–10 games.
  • Market lines are sharp. Edges under 2–3% are usually noise. Track your results in Track record before trusting the model with real money.
  • Your teams, settings and log are saved to your account, so they follow you across devices.
For entertainment and analysis. No model beats the market reliably, and past results don't guarantee future ones. If you bet, bet only what you can afford to lose.
